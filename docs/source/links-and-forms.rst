Commonly Used Links and Forms
=============================

Links & Sites
-------------

- `Scanner schedule: <https://www.sistat.ucla.edu/CCNSAS/login.asp/>`_ You do not need to log in to SIStat to view the calendar. This is also bookmarked on the control room iMac.
- `SIStat system login: <https://www.sistat.ucla.edu/CCNSAS/ShowCalendar.asp#/>`_ Use SIStat to manage scan scheduling operations, including booking scans, adding lab personnel, checking certification status, linking funds, and uploading IRBs.
-  `BruinLearn: <https://bruinlearn.ucla.edu/>`_ This platform hosts the materials and exams required for safety certification and recertification.
- `CCN Website: <https://www.staglincenterforcogneuro.semel.ucla.edu/>`_ CCN's main, public-facing site. Because the site lists active labs and projects, please notify us of any changes to your `lab information <https://forms.gle/FKzH4nXrX2x8RKtM6>`_ or `active projects <https://forms.gle/yBmxgFYyQshUJW9TA>`_.
- `CCN YouTube channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@uclastaglincenterforcognit4262/videos/>`_ The channel contains videos demonstrating common CCN procedures, including preparing a participant for a scan, navigating the upgraded XA30 software, and placing voxels for spectroscopy.


Forms
-----

- `Metal Screening Form: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1aUwmgXoij_czd8YktXmo0yDLjpqbuUpm/view?usp=sharing/>`_ **REQUIRED** for any person entering the scanner. “Review the form carefully and submit documentation for any concerns `as a ticket <https://docs.google.com/document/d/13g-DVRauCgZAkScw0D5j03cCPeCiu2Fi/edit#heading=h.rgb25in7p2nc>`_.
- `Illness Screening Form: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kXsq_b25apk08NTTJ82GWX4adjsyz_iq/view?usp=sharing/>`_ Must be administered the evening before or morning of the participant's scan appointment. Reschedule your participant if they show signs of illness.
- `Pre-Scan Form: <https://uclahs.az1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6tI8o3SvorTot9k/>`_ Fill out this brief form at the beginning of every scan. It is bookmarked on the iMac.
- `Post-Scan Form: <https://uclahs.az1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3BQesXgv9K8kgPc/>`_ Fill out this brief form after each scan. It is bookmarked on the iMac.
- `Scan Cancellation Form: <https://forms.gle/E9uzNYDZheAqihce7/>`_ Fill out this form if you need to cancel a scan within 72 hours. You must also cancel your scan in SIStat.
- `Pilot Application Form: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rhfezXO3QP4pV5yjUnNmI8LNkjw7EqS9/view?usp=drive_link/>`_ Complete this form to apply for scan hours for a pilot project and send it to alenarto@g.ucla.edu. Please report the outcome of the pilot, including whether it contributed to securing funding; this information is important for maintaining the pilot program


Manuals
-------

- `CCN Safety Manual: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ocMVWpSLnR-oUUMrK7BbQakE5fyiw9OU/view?usp=sharing>`_ Covers emergency devices and procedures.
- `CCN Operations Manual: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dRt_A6ZsFMJwupx9PPbaWokV70NhxesR/view?usp=sharing>`_ Covers reboots, save logs, and incidental findings.
- `Best Practices for Appointment Management: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1yLR77cLHod_-bwauu4AtoAq51bbiNUgv/view?usp=sharing>`_ A list of recommendations for minimizing late cancellations.
- `MR Safety Considerations: <https://drive.google.com/file/d/17ntnhvzU91m7uJYVULiTRkr-u77_ioSt/view?usp=sharing>`_ A compilation of important considerations when screening a potential participant for MRI, including topics that are frequently overlooked or that participants may forget to mention.
